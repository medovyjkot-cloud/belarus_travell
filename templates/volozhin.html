Volozhin City & Surroundings

Volozhin is a charming town located on the Volozhinka River, about 76 km away from Minsk. Known since the 14th century, it was historical property of famous noble families: the Radziwills, the Czartoryskis, and the Counts Tyszkiewicz.

🏰 Key Historical Landmarks

Tyszkiewicz Palace Complex

A monument of classicist architecture from the early 19th century, which once included a palace, a greenhouse, and a beautiful English park.

St. Joseph's Church

A grand Neoclassical Catholic church built in 1816. It features a majestic portico with six columns and statues of the Evangelists.

Volozhin Yeshiva

The world-renowned Jewish theological seminary, known as the "Mother of Yeshivas," opened in 1803. It attracted students from all over the globe.

📜 Legends of Volozhin

The Legend of Tyszkiewicz's Underground Tunnels

Local folklore says that a huge network of secret underground labyrinths is hidden beneath the Volozhin Palace. According to the legend, one of the main tunnels was so wide that Count Tyszkiewicz could easily ride through it in a carriage drawn by three horses. It is believed that this path led several kilometers outside the town directly to the family chapel, and priceless treasures are still buried somewhere inside its walls.
