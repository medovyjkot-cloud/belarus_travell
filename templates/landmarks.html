Famous Landmarks of Belarus

Explore the most iconic historical sites, majestic castles, and hero monuments that define the rich heritage of Belarus.

Mir Castle Complex

A UNESCO World Heritage site and an outstanding example of 16th-century fortification architecture. It beautifully combines Gothic, Renaissance, and Baroque styles.

Brest Hero-Fortress

A world-famous symbol of Soviet resistance during World War II. The memorial complex features the colossal "Courage" monument and the iconic main entrance shaped like a star.

Nesvizh Palace and Park

The residential castle of the Radziwill family, also protected by UNESCO. Surrounded by beautiful lakes and English landscape gardens, it is a true gem of Belarus.
